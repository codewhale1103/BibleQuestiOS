# Noah’s ocean voyage artwork

The seven original chapter stops become a scrollable ocean journey: sheltered shores, flood and storm waters, then a dove and a rainbow near the Ararat-inspired island. The islands are map landmarks and artistic scenery, rather than a reconstruction of the flood geography. Native SwiftUI Canvas draws moving swell lines, traveling foam crests, water glints, geographic rain, seabirds, the rainbow and the ark’s wake. The ark follows one shared Bézier route with the chapter beacons and scroll anchor.

All five images were generated with the built-in image generation tool and saved inside the project. No image generation or external asset service is required at runtime.

## Implementation

`NoahVoyageRoute.swift` is the source of positions, tangents, headings and chapter descriptions. `NoahOceanScenery.swift` renders the sea, weather and land. `NoahQuestMap.swift` layers the route, nautical markers and floating ark; `NoahVoyageInterface.swift` contains the header, dock controls and chapter story sheets. `NoahAdventureView.swift` manages sailing, scrolling and local position restoration. Map exploration does not write game-completion or reward data to Firebase. Existing David screens and shared adventure drawing helpers are preserved.

Water drawing is restricted to the visible map region; offscreen ark animation pauses. Reduce Motion freezes decorative animation and moves directly to selected stops. Opening a chapter, leaving the map, rotating the layout or backgrounding the app stops sailing. Start returns to the first chapter; Latest returns to the farthest locally visited position.
